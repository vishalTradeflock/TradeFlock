Acme Agrees to Buy Northwind for $4.2 Billion

NEW YORK. Acme Corp said on Tuesday it agreed to buy Northwind Industries for $4.2 billion in cash and stock, ending a two-month auction that drew three other bids.

Chief executive Lena Ortiz said the price is 18% above Northwind's closing share price on Friday. The deal values Northwind at $62 a share, Acme said in a statement.

Northwind, based in Chicago, reported $9.1 billion of revenue last year and $1.4 billion of operating income. Acme said it expects the combination to add to earnings in the first full year after closing.

Breakup fee and timing

Acme will pay a $210 million breakup fee if regulators block the deal. The companies said they expect to close in the first half of next year, subject to a vote by Northwind shareholders and clearance from the Federal Trade Commission.

Ortiz said Acme will keep Northwind's Chicago headquarters and its brand. About 1,200 Northwind employees work at that office, the company said.

How the auction ended

A European bidder dropped out last week after its financing bank reduced a commitment by $800 million. Acme's board approved the offer on Monday night, Ortiz said.
